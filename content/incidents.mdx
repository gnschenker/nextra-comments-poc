# Incidents

Page the platform on-call for customer-facing errors, data-loss risk, or a deploy that cannot be rolled back.

## Before you page

Please review the runbook before paging. Check the status dashboard, the last deploy thread, and whether a rollback is already running.

## During an incident

Keep one written timeline in the incident channel. Note when you started the canary, when you rolled back, and who you paged.

## After the incident

Later, review the runbook after the incident. Add the missing step on the same day while the details are still sharp.

The follow-up pull request should name the user-visible failure, the detection gap, and the change that prevents a repeat. Link it from the incident ticket.
