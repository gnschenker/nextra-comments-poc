# Platform handbook

This handbook is the working guide for the internal developer platform. It covers how teams request environments, ship services, and respond when something breaks.

Use the comments at the bottom of each page for questions that apply to the whole document. Select a sentence in the article when the note belongs on that sentence.

## Who this is for

Platform engineers, service owners, and on-call responders. If you are joining the platform team, start with [Getting started](/getting-started). If you already have a service, skip to [Deployments](/deploy).

## How we work

Changes to this handbook go through the same review path as product code. Two reviewers is the default. Urgent incident notes can land first and be cleaned up the next business day.

Keep examples generic. Do not paste customer names, account ids, or production secrets into these pages or into comments.
